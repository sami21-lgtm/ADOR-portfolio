About Me

Cinematic Storytelling
Through Design

I’m Ador, an EEE student and visual designer based in Dhaka, passionate about cinematic storytelling through design. My work focuses on poster design, sports media visuals, and editorial-style digital creatives that combine emotion, typography, and strong composition.

Over time, I’ve worked with football and cricket-based media platforms such as Futbol and Howzat, creating engaging social media visuals designed to capture energy, narrative, and audience attention. I currently serve as the Design Head at RADS, contributing to the visual direction and branding of the platform through modern, aesthetically driven design solutions.

Beyond design, my background in Electrical and Electronic Engineering helps shape a structured and analytical approach to creativity — balancing visual experimentation with purposeful design thinking.
